One of *Password*'s central mechanics is carrying information across timelines. Some passwords are learned from bad endings and revisited through **Resonate?**, while others are hinted at before the input itself.

In b0.85, four password checks have a major effect on the main story:

1. Day 4 (D4) or Day 6 (D6), depending on the character route
2. Day 7 (D7)
3. Day 10 (D10)
4. Day 17 (D17)

The final Path P sequence also contains a simpler standalone password. D1 and D3 have optional inputs, while D2 allows an attempt but has no valid password.

::: {.callout-important}
## How to use this page

This guide provides progressive hints rather than revealing the key passwords outright.

Start with Tier 1. Open Tier 2 only when necessary, then use Tier 3 as the final step. In the letter patterns, each `_` represents one hidden letter.

Every password covered by the four key checks and the final Path P sequence is a single English word. Input is not case-sensitive, but the spelling must be correct.
:::

## First key password group

**Input time:**

- Roswell Route: D4 afternoon
- All other character routes: D6 morning

Each of the six character routes has its own password. You must enter the word associated with your current route to receive the warning needed to avoid that route's early failure.

Entering another character's valid password can show the warning vision associated with that character, but it does not protect the character on your current route.

<details>
<summary><strong>Tier 1 hint</strong></summary>

The clearest clue for each password appears in that character's early bad ending.

These passwords are designed around information carried back from a failed timeline. After seeing the relevant failure, use **Resonate?** to return to the earlier password check.
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Tier 2 hint</strong></summary>

Look for a word that is repeated, unusually emphasized, or written in all capitals during the failure timeline.

The word usually relates directly to the cause of that character's death or danger.

For Tyson's clue, choose **Open it.** when the letter appears. Choosing **Ignore it.** skips the explicit word.
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Tier 3: letter patterns</strong></summary>

- Dean: `d _ _ _ h c _ p`
- Tyson: `s _ l _ d _ r _ t y`
- Roswell: `d _ s c _ v _ r y`
- Orlando: `s _ n t _ n c _`
- Hoss: `h _ r _ i s m`
- Sal: `d r _ _ m _ r`
</details>

::: {.callout-note}
For Roswell, entering the correct password reveals the warning but does not automatically save him. You must still make the appropriate choice later in the sequence.
:::

## Second key password

**Input time:** D7 evening

Only one password is accepted here in b0.85.

<details>
<summary><strong>Tier 1 hint</strong></summary>

Allow the password check to fail and continue into the Benson bad ending.

The clearest clue appears in the conversations surrounding Benson's death. **Resonate?** then returns you to the D7 Vault.
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Tier 2 hint</strong></summary>

Notice how the characters describe the mental state required to commit the act, and how they characterize what someone did to Benson.

The same word connects both ideas.
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Tier 3 hint</strong></summary>

The word describes the act of turning against someone who trusted you.

**Letter pattern:** `b _ t r _ y _ l`
</details>

::: {.callout-note}
The two older candidate words are not accepted passwords in b0.85, even though traces of the earlier design remain visible.
:::

## Third key password

**Input time:** D10 evening

<details>
<summary><strong>Tier 1 hint</strong></summary>

The word is spoken several times before the password check.

Outside the Sal Route, revealing Oswin changes which character is involved in part of the conversation, but it does not change the password.
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Tier 2 hint</strong></summary>

The same word can summarize the disaster that unfolds on the Path C/D side after this check is missed.
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Tier 3 hint</strong></summary>

The word describes the deliberate killing of many people in a single event.

**Letter pattern:** `m _ s s _ c r _`
</details>

### Sal Route exception

Giving up on this password during the Sal Route does not move the run to the ordinary Path C/D side. The earlier intervention in Sal's sleep-related problem prevents that branch.

The password is still important for completion, however. Entering it correctly displays the Sal vault CG and a related image recorded in the death Gallery. Other Sal Gallery content is obtained through normal route progression and does not depend on this password.

## Fourth key password

**Input time:** D17 afternoon

<details>
<summary><strong>Tier 1 hint</strong></summary>

Focus on Orlando's wording.

During the Vault sequence, the correct memory prompt is **Orlando said it.** Tyson's dialogue is relevant to the situation, but it is not the direct source of the answer.
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Tier 2 hint</strong></summary>

The word describes the form associated with Tyson after he loses control.

Entering `monster` or `feral` makes Thanatos tell you that you are close.
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Tier 3 hint</strong></summary>

**Letter pattern:** `w _ r _ w _ l f`

The answer names a legendary human who transforms into a wolf-like creature, usually under a full moon.
</details>

### Tyson Route exception

Failing this check on the Tyson Route does not ultimately divert the run into Path F/G. This protection occurs later and does not count as solving the D17 password.

For the full branch logic, see [Lettered Path System](path-system.md).

## Final Path P password

The final Path P sequence contains a standalone password that is separate from the ordinary day-based Vault system. Its clue is presented immediately before the input, and an incorrect answer automatically returns to the same prompt.

<details>
<summary><strong>Tier 1 hint</strong></summary>

Read Thanatos's wording immediately before the keyboard prompt.
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Tier 2 and Tier 3 hints</strong></summary>

The word means the process of **deifying someone** or **elevating a mortal to divine status**.

You do not need information from another Path or bad ending.
</details>

## Optional inputs and residual strings

The early-game Vault contains several inputs that are not required for normal progression:

- D1 has an implemented hidden or meta password.
- D2 allows the player to enter text, but no answer is accepted on that day.
- D3 has an implemented optional password that increases affection for all six characters.
- Some obsolete password strings remain in b0.85 but are not accepted inputs.
- A D11 word is shown and defined in the story, but it is not a usable password in b0.85.

These items are not part of the four route-critical password checks.

## Retry behavior

A normal wrong answer does not immediately display **Resonate?**

- D3 and D6 use **Try Another Password**.
- D4, D7, D10, and D17 use **Try Again**.
- The corresponding exit option is **Give up**.
- **Resonate?** appears after certain bad endings or Path endpoints and sends the player back to an earlier password node.
- The final Path P password automatically repeats after an incorrect answer and has no exit menu.

## Still stuck?

Check the following:

1. Are you playing b0.85?
2. Are you following instructions written for b0.7 or an earlier version?
3. Is the spelling exact? Capitalization does not matter, and leading or trailing spaces are removed automatically.
4. Are you entering the word on the correct day?
5. For the first password group, are you using the word associated with your current character route?
6. Have you mistaken a failed-timeline clue for something that should appear before the first attempt?

When reporting an apparent mismatch, include your character route, current day, displayed Path, and most recent major choice.
